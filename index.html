Why Solar Energy

Understand Solar Before You Invest

Solar is a long-term energy decision. Here is what it actually does — and what it depends on.

  1. 01

    Renewable energy from sunlight

    Solar panels convert daylight into electricity — a renewable source that supports cleaner energy use.

  2. 02

    Daytime electricity generation

    Your system produces power during daylight hours, when many homes and businesses use the most electricity.

  3. 03

    Possible reduction in grid usage

    Solar may reduce how much electricity you draw from the grid. Results vary with usage patterns, system size and conditions.

  4. 04

    Planning for long-term needs

    A well-planned system can be designed to accommodate future requirements and upgrades.
